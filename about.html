About Kii

Kii is a Johannesburg-based artist bringing together emotion, storytelling and sound inspired by the culture of the West Rand of Jozi.

Blending modern production with raw artistic expression, creating music that represents identity, experience and movement.

From the streets of Randfontein to streaming platforms worldwide, Kii's sound is unmistakably his own — layered, honest, and built for the culture.
